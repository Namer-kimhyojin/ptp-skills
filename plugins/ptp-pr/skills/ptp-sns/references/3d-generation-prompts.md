# 테오·피오 3D 생성 프롬프트 세트

아래 프롬프트는 장면 설명 앞에 붙이는 정체성·스타일 계약이다. 실제 작업에서는 목적에 맞는 장면, 행동, 카메라 구도를 추가하되 불변값은 삭제하지 않는다.

## 테오 단독

```text
Input references: the approved Teo 2D master sheet is the identity authority; the Teo 3D style sheet is the volume, material and lighting reference.
Render the same approved Teo character as a premium rounded soft-vinyl animated institutional mascot. Preserve the exact asymmetrical blue bob/wave head silhouette, white oval face, simple dark oval eyes, approximately 2.7-head proportions, rounded torso, limb thickness, simple white hands, broad rounded shoes and approved white one-piece outfit with blue shoulder/side panels, blue wrist trim and blue soles. The only symbol is one small blue uppercase T at the screen-right upper chest, embroidery-sized.
Materials: warm matte white face, semi-matte white suit with extremely subtle textile grain, restrained satin blue head and trim, rubber-like blue soles, no metallic or glass finish. Lighting: soft neutral three-point daylight, subtle ambient occlusion and contact shadow. Keep natural 50–70mm perspective without body distortion.
No head mark, antenna, ribbon, central large letter, jacket, navy pants, skirt, separate gloves, wrist device, extra logo, watermark or costume redesign.
```

## 테오·피오 동시 등장

```text
Input references: the approved Teo and Pio 2D master sheets are the identity authorities; the paired 3D family sheet is the body, material and lighting reference.
Render Teo and Pio together as the same premium rounded soft-vinyl animated mascot family. Keep both approximately 2.7 heads tall with the same shoulder-width range, rounded torso volume, limb thickness, hand size and shoe size. Pio must not be slimmer or taller. Preserve each approved head silhouette, face, default one-piece outfit and color panels. Only the small blue T for Teo and small orange P for Pio may appear at the screen-right upper chest.
Use identical material roughness and soft neutral three-point daylight for both characters: warm matte white faces, semi-matte white suits with subtle textile grain, restrained satin colored head shells and trim, rubber-like colored soles, subtle ambient occlusion and grounded contact shadows. Match scene light direction and color temperature without changing the materials into metal, glass or neon.
No head marks, central large letters, extra icons, costume redesign, mismatched body mass, extreme wide-angle distortion, duplicate limbs, props with logos, in-image copy or watermark.
```
